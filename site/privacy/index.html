Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

OwnBooks is an app for listening to and reading your own DRM-free audiobooks and ebooks. Your books stay on your device. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the OwnBooks app and these web pages, why, and what rights you have.

Who we are

The data controller is AB Equinoxe Marketing Oy (Business ID 3564918-4), Aurantie 2B, 45100 Kouvola, Finland, publishing as Equinoxe Apps.

For anything about privacy, write to privacy@equinoxe.app. For help with the app, write to support@equinoxe.app.

The short version

  • Your book files and covers never leave your device. We never upload them.
  • We store your account and the minimum needed to keep your place in sync across your devices: which books are in your library (title, author, a fingerprint of the file) and how far you are.
  • Analytics and crash reports are off unless you turn them on, and never include what you read.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and all synced data at any time, in the app or on the web.

What we process and why

Your account

You need an account to use OwnBooks. We store your email address, the sign-in method you used, your password if you chose email and password (only as a secure hash), and when the account was created and last signed in.

If you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook, that provider tells us your email address and an account identifier. With Apple you can choose to hide your email; we then receive a relay address from Apple. For Facebook sign-in, our server checks the sign-in with Facebook and asks it for your Facebook user ID and email address; we do not receive your friends, posts or other profile data.

We send you emails only to run your account: a code to confirm a new account and a code to reset your password.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Sync data

To keep your library and your place the same on all your devices, we store:

  • Your books' details: title, author, type (audiobook or ebook), length, and a fingerprint. The fingerprint is a code calculated on your device from the file's size and its first and last megabyte, so the same file can be recognised on another device without uploading it.
  • Your progress: your position in each book, playback speed, whether you finished it, when it last changed, and which device it came from.
  • Your devices: a random device identifier, the device's name as set on the device (which can include your name, such as "Alex's phone") or otherwise its model, its platform (Android or iOS) and when it last synced.

When you remove a book from all your devices, we delete its progress and keep a removal marker with the book's details, so your other devices remove it too. Removal markers are deleted with your account.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

What stays on your device

Your book files, cover images, reading and listening settings, and your answers to the questions when you first open the app stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Analytics and crash reports (optional)

The app asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. They are off until you agree, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's settings. If you agree, we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn which features are used (for example that an import finished, or how long a listening session was, in broad ranges) and to fix crashes. These reports include technical details such as the device model, operating system and app version, and an identifier created for the app installation.

They never contain book titles, authors, file names or fingerprints, or anything else about what you read or listen to. Advertising identifiers are not collected and ad personalisation is off.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Security and operations

Our servers keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

These web pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The pages use no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. The account deletion page stores your sign-in session in your browser only while you use it.

How long we keep data

  • Account and sync data: until you delete your account, then immediately.
  • Database backups: deleted data can remain in our provider's backups for up to 7 days before they are overwritten.
  • Analytics data (if you agreed): up to 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Server logs: a few days, at most 90 days.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. Without the app, use the account deletion page. Either way, your account and all synced data are deleted immediately. If you can't sign in, email privacy@equinoxe.app from the address on your account.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, deleted or restricted, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email privacy@equinoxe.app. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

OwnBooks is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters for how we use your data, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
